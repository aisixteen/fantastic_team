# cli_ssh/precreated_1_2

The ssh twin of `cli/precreated_1_2` (#242, #252): one task split across the local kernel (the master) and a remote one in a Podman box, wired the weak way.

The lead, `w1` and `w2` on the master (made before the task), and `w3`, kept for the call in flight below. `w1` fixes adder.py and pushes. The remote agent (the work program in the box) sends `w2` its work and watches it: `w2`'s turn runs on the master, its status and parts stream back to the box, and its tools are the box's exec agent (pull, fix muler.py, push). This is the RECONNECT twin: the remote kernel and the tunnel are separate jobs, and between the lead's two messages the operator seals the watch grant (the box's watch is refused: the mutation), breaks the tunnel while a call to `w3` is in flight (it fails `bridge_down` on the box at once; to a backend of its own, because the call aborted by the drop leaves its backend `thinking`, `docs/fantastic-notes.md`), makes the tunnel again and has the master redial the known port (the bridge's `reconnect`: a ws bridge does not redial by itself), checks that the next call through the bridge answers, and that a watcher the box made before the break goes on streaming a turn of the master's on `w2` with no new `watch`. The lead then reads `w2`'s history back and closes (message 2).

Checks: The local checks of `cli/precreated_1_2`; each worker asked the model, both on the master; the work program exited 0; `w2`'s history (its push) came back; and the reconnect phase's checks above.

The wiring, the shared checks (`Box.end`) and what the twin needs (Podman, else `SKIP`; builds the Linux binary once per source tree) are the twins' in `e2e/README.md` ("The weak wiring", "The twins"). The scripted plug's `script.json` is written by `python3 -m helpers.ssh_twins --write` in `e2e/`; the fake forge, no network.
